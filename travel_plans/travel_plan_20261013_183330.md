1. **Train tickets** – Book **non‑AC Sleeper** if you’re comfortable; saves ~₹ 400 each way.  
2. **Breakfast** – Most budget hotels include it; skip café breakfasts.  
3. **Meals** – Eat at **dhabas, chaat stalls, and small eateries**; a full meal can be under ₹ 150.  
4. **Transport** – Use **Lucknow Metro** for longer hops (₹ 20‑₹ 40 per ride). For short distances, negotiate auto‑rickshaw fares **before boarding**.  
5. **Attractions** – Many monuments have **free entry on certain days** (e.g., Tuesdays for some museums). Check online before you go.  
6. **Shopping** – Bargain politely in markets; start at 30‑40 % lower than the asking price.
