## DETAILED TIPS TO STAY **UNDER ₹10,000**

### 1. **Transport**
- **Train over flight** – the non‑AC sleeper is the cheapest & gives you a night’s rest en‑route.
- **Delhi Metro** – buy a **30‑day smart card** (₹100 deposit + ₹60 per day) → ₹1,200 total, cheaper than multiple day‑passes.
- **Auto‑rickshaws & shared cabs** – always ask for the fare before starting; negotiate for ₹30‑₹50 for short hops.

### 2. **Accommodation**
| Hotel / Hostel | Location | Approx. Nightly Rate (₹) | Why it fits the budget |
|----------------|----------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| **Zostel Delhi** (Paharganj) | Near New Delhi Metro (Kashmere Gate) | 500 (dorm) / 800 (private) | Clean, social, free Wi‑Fi, lockers |
| **OYO 12345** (Karol Bagh) | Close to Metro, cheap food stalls | 450 (single) | Basic but safe; easy access to Connaught Place |
| **Hostel 7** (North Campus) | Near Delhi University | 400 (dorm) | Very low cost, good for solo travelers |
> **Book** via **Hostelworld**, **OYO app**, or **MakeMyTrip**. Look for “Free cancellation” to stay flexible.

### 3. **Food Hacks**
- **Breakfast:** Street stalls (parathas, poha, idli) – ₹30‑₹80.
- **Lunch:** *Thali* or *chaat* stalls – ₹100‑₹150.
- **Dinner:** Small restaurants in **Connaught Place**, **Karim’s**, **Dilli Haat** – ₹150‑₹250.
- **Water:** Carry a 1‑liter reusable bottle; refill at free water dispensers in metro stations.

### 4. **Entry Fees (All INR)**
| Attraction | Fee (Indian) | Fee (Foreign) |
|------------|--------------|---------------|
| Red Fort | 35 | 500 |
| Humayun’s Tomb | 30 | 500 |
| Taj Mahal (Day) | 50 | 1,300 |
| Agra Fort | 30 | 550 |
| Qutub Minar | 30 | 600 |
| India Gate, Lotus Temple, Lodhi Gardens | Free | – |
> If you’re an Indian citizen, you stay comfortably under the budget. For foreign nationals, the total would rise ~₹2,500‑₹3,000, still possible with slight adjustments (e.g., stay in a hostel instead of private room).

### 5. **Safety & Practicalities**
- **Carry a photocopy of your ID & train ticket** at all times.
- **Use only registered auto‑rickshaws** (yellow plates) or app‑based rides.
- **Keep cash in small denominations**; many street vendors don’t accept cards.
- **Stay in well‑lit areas** (Paharganj, Karol Bagh, Connaught Place) after dark.
- **Internet:** Most hostels and cafes provide free Wi‑Fi; consider a cheap prepaid data pack (₹200 for 2 GB).
